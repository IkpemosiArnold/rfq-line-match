Checking

A line is ready when its words fit exactly one catalog item, every word on it was read, its unit turns into the unit the item is sold in, and its price sits on a row that is still in date. A customer part number has to agree with the words.

Linear feet become pieces by the item's length and square feet become sheets by the sheet size, rounded up, with the sum shown. Delivery is added only when the email asks for it and its ZIP is on the zone sheet. Anything else is held with the reason and the choices.
